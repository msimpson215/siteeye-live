# A1 — Building the Brain
## 20 questions to ask Joe

**How to run it:** Four sessions, five questions each. About 15 minutes per
session. Joe talks, phone records. He does it from the driver's seat.

**Rule:** Never ask for policy. Ask for stories and specific incidents.
"What are your quality standards?" gets you nothing.
"Tell me about the last job that went bad" gets you everything.

---

### SESSION 1 — THE NEW GUY
*Impetus: "Joe, a kid shows up Monday who's never held a squeegee. Talk me through him."*

1. He pulls into the yard Monday at 6am. Where does he go, who does he find,
   what should he have brought with him?
2. Walk me through every tool on the truck. Name it, and tell me what it's for.
3. What are the things a new guy does in week one that drive you crazy?
4. What's the first job you'd let him do by himself? What's the last?
5. How do you know when a guy is ready to run his own crew?

---

### SESSION 2 — HOW THE WORK GETS DONE
*Impetus: "Pretend I'm standing next to you on a seal job and I've never seen one."*

6. Walk me through a seal job start to finish. Every step, in order.
7. Same thing for striping. Same for crack fill. What's the sequence?
8. What temperature and what weather will you NOT work in — and why?
9. How long before a car can drive on it? How do you actually keep them off?
10. What's the difference between a job you're proud of and one that's just done?

---

### SESSION 3 — WHEN IT GOES WRONG
*Impetus: "Tell me about the jobs that cost you money."*

11. Tell me about the last job that went bad. What happened, start to finish?
12. What's the most expensive mistake you've ever had to eat?
13. Sealer beading up, not curing, lifting — what causes each one, what do you do?
14. The customer walks out mid-job and starts complaining. What do you say?
15. What's the callback you get more than any other?

---

### SESSION 4 — JUDGMENT AND MONEY
*Impetus: "This is the stuff that's only in your head."*

16. **What do your guys call you about the most? List every question you got
    this week.** ← the single most valuable question here
17. What can a foreman decide on his own? What has to come to you, always?
18. How do you talk to a property manager differently than a homeowner?
19. What makes a job profitable versus one where you lose your shirt?
20. If you were unreachable for a week, what would you need your crew to know?

---

## Notes

- **Q16 is the goldmine.** It's the actual curriculum. Everything else is
  supporting material. If Joe only answers one question, make it this one.
- Let him ramble. The detours are where the real knowledge is.
- Every answer that starts "well, it depends" — stop and dig. That's judgment,
  and judgment is the part that's hard to replace.
- After launch the crew's questions feed this list. It writes itself from there.
