Why SiteEye 360 Exists

The owner or supervisor cannot be at every job site at the same time.

SITEEYE 360

SiteEye 360 was created from a simple problem contractors deal with every day: the owner or supervisor cannot be at every job site at the same time.

A crew may be miles away. A new employee may have a question. A customer may question whether the work was completed correctly. A job that should take hours can stretch into days. And when something goes wrong, the owner is often left trying to determine what happened after the fact.

SiteEye 360 gives owners and supervisors a live view of the job while the work is happening.

From a phone or computer, management can check progress, verify workmanship, zoom in when something needs attention, and communicate with the crew before a small problem becomes an expensive one.

It also creates valuable documentation. If there is a customer dispute, property damage, safety incident, or question about how a job was performed, SiteEye 360 can provide a clearer record of what actually occurred.

With the advanced AI version, SiteEye 360 can become more than a camera. The AI team member can be trained on the company’s procedures, equipment, materials, and standards so employees can ask questions from the job site without having to call the owner every time they need guidance.

SiteEye 360 is not about watching employees. It is about giving owners better visibility into their business, helping crews do the job correctly, protecting the company, and giving everyone the information they need while the job is still happening.
