FIRST — WHAT 4G AND 5G ACTUALLY ARE
5G is not zoom. Zoom, pan and tilt are the camera.
4G and 5G are the pipe that sends the live video to your phone

Same idea as your phone. 4G and 5G are both cellular. 5G is a faster, bigger pipe. Neither one turns the camera or zooms in. The camera does that.
