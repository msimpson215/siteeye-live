SITEEYE 360 BRANDED

1. The tower — every unit starts here

DEWALT tripod. Pull the light head off. Mount the camera on an extension. Power it from the lithium batteries already on the pole. Wire the camera feed down through that extension.

1
DEWALT tripod — mast and base
DEWALT DCL079R1 20V MAX Tripod Light Kit
  • Rugged collapsible job-site tripod
  • Extends to 7 ft
  • Includes 20V MAX 6Ah lithium battery and charger
BUILD STEPS
  1. Buy the DEWALT tripod light kit
  2. Take the light head off
  3. Add the camera extension (about 3 ft)
  4. Mount the camera on top
  5. Run power and signal wire through the extension from the battery pack below
Final camera height approximately 10 ft
