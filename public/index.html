FAQ

Is this a security camera?

No. Security cameras stay on a building. SiteEye goes with your crew for the workday, then packs up.

Do I need job-site Wi‑Fi?

No. It runs on cellular (4G/5G) so you can watch from your phone or office anywhere there’s signal.
