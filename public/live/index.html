No Reolink yet. Your smartphone is the camera. Joe can watch from 20 miles away — he does not need your Wi-Fi, and he does not download an app.

  1. On the smartphone, open Camera, tap Start, allow camera and mic, point it. Tap Add SiteEye to this phone so it has an icon.
  2. Text Joe the Watch link and the 4-letter code. He can be at his house.
  3. On Joe’s phone, open Watch, type the code, tap Add SiteEye to this phone. He gets a home-screen icon. No Play Store.
Camera The phone on the pole. Stands in for the yellow SiteEye unit. Watch — this is the app Joe’s phone. Dashboard, live view, grid, PTZ, Talk, Light.
