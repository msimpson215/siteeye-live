SiteEye 360 LIVE Prototype demo

Show Joe it works.

No Reolink yet. Your smartphone is the camera on the pole. His phone or a laptop is the office watching the job.

  1. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi (Joe’s house is fine).
  2. On the smartphone, open Camera and tap Start. Allow the camera. Point it at the work.
  3. On the laptop or Joe’s phone, open Watch and type the 4-letter code from the camera.
Camera Use this on the smartphone. This stands in for the Reolink until you have one. Watch Use this on a laptop or Joe’s phone. Live picture, pinch or tap + to look closer.

There is nothing to download from an app store. Text Joe this page. On the phone, Chrome menu (three dots) → Add to Home screen. SiteEye then sits on the phone like an app.

Two laptops work the same way: one uses Camera (webcam), the other uses Watch. Canva art is optional — the live picture is the demo. If you make a PNG in Canva, drop it in this chat and I will put it on the page. This is not on the public SiteEye homepage.
