Show Joe it works.

This is the app from your artwork — dark screen, gold, live cameras. No Reolink yet. Your smartphone is the camera. Joe’s phone is the office.

  1. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the smartphone, open Camera, tap Start, allow the camera, point it.
  3. On Joe’s phone, open Watch, type the 4-letter code.
  4. On Joe’s phone: Chrome menu (three dots) → Add to Home screen. Nothing to download from the Play Store.
Camera The phone on the pole. Stands in for the yellow SiteEye unit. Watch — this is the app Joe’s phone. Dashboard, live view, grid, PTZ, Talk, Light.
