OPTIONAL ADD-ON

110V Generator or Wall Plug Input

Only needed on long, multi-day or lit jobs where the battery has to be topped off without leaving the site. GFCI protected, and it plugs straight into the same charging port. If the site already has power, or the unit goes home at night to charge, skip it entirely — it is not part of the budget below.
